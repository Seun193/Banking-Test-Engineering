# Feature 1001 — Domestic Money Transfer

## Business Goal

As a retail banking customer,
I want to transfer money from one of my eligible accounts to another domestic bank account,
so that I can make payments securely through digital banking.

## Business Requirements

- Authenticated retail customers can create a domestic money transfer.
- The customer selects an eligible debit account.
- The customer provides a beneficiary account number.
- The customer provides a transfer amount in EUR.
- The customer may provide an optional payment message.
- A transfer must not be completed when required information is invalid.
- Successful transfers must return a confirmation to the customer.

## Out of Scope

- International transfers.
- Scheduled recurring transfers.
- Foreign-currency transfers.
